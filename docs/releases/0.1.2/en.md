BLACKWALL 0.1.2 — Android.

### What is new
- Android 10 and later: the first build of the app. Routes per application (through the VPN only, direct, no network), reserve servers, apps held after Disconnect, and a notification with the server, the time connected and the speed. It comes back by itself after a restart of the phone and works with Android's "Always-on VPN" and "Block connections without VPN". The interface is in English, Russian and Chinese in one file.
- macOS: a new app icon, drawn for macOS 26.
- A privacy policy: [docs/privacy.md](../../blob/main/docs/privacy.md). Nothing is sent to the developer.

The build is not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. macOS: right-click the app → Open. Windows: SmartScreen → More info → Run anyway. Android: open the APK and allow installing from that source.

The app is also coming to Google Play. The APK from here and the app from Google Play are signed differently, so one does not update the other: to switch, uninstall first.
