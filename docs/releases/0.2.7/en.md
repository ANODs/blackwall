BLACKWALL 0.2.7: subscriptions live on the servers screen.

### What is new
- Subscriptions moved from the settings to the servers screen: under a subscription's name, its provider's note, without emoji or blank lines.
- A subscription has a ⋯ menu: refresh, copy link, contact support, remove.
- A server has a right-click menu (a long press on Android): copy link, copy JSON for Xray, remove a key added by hand. An "Auto" group copies all its links at once, and its JSON comes with a leastPing balancer.
