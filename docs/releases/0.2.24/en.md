BLACKWALL 0.2.24: connections look like today's Chrome, sites of Russia and the CIS go direct, settings are easier to read.

### What is new
- Connections to servers now look like Chrome 155. On some Russian networks a connection that looked like an older Chrome was cut, and after a few of them the server stopped answering from that network for hours.
- Sites of Russia and the CIS open around the VPN, also in apps with a VPN rule, so they never see the server's address. On by default, the switch is Settings → Routes → Russia and the CIS direct. A rule you set for a site comes first. The list is updated daily.
- macOS, Windows, Linux: the rules for apps and sites, together with where other apps go, can be copied to the clipboard, loaded from it or reset (Settings → Routes → Rules).
- Settings are grouped into Connection, Protection, Routes and App, and rare technical options are folded under Advanced. Every option has a clearer name and a short description. Saved settings stay as they were.
